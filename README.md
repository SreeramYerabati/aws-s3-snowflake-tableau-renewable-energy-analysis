# Renewable Energy Consumption Analytics | AWS S3, Snowflake SQL & Tableau

## Project Overview

This project demonstrates an end-to-end cloud analytics solution designed to analyze renewable energy consumption patterns and associated cost savings across multiple countries, regions, and energy sources.

Data was stored in Amazon S3, ingested into Snowflake using Storage Integrations and External Stages, transformed using SQL, and visualized through interactive Tableau dashboards published to Tableau Cloud.

The objective was to identify consumption trends, evaluate energy source performance, and measure financial savings generated through renewable energy adoption.

## Business Questions Addressed

• Which regions consume the highest amount of renewable energy?

• Which countries have the highest renewable energy usage?

• Which renewable energy sources contribute the most energy production?

• Which regions generate the highest cost savings?

• Which countries realize the greatest economic benefit from renewable energy adoption?

• How do income levels impact energy consumption and cost savings?

## Data Transformation

Scenario-based transformations were performed to simulate changes in energy consumption and financial outcomes across different income levels.

### Monthly Usage Adjustments

• Low Income: +10%

• Middle Income: +20%

• High Income: +30%

### Cost Savings Adjustments

• Low Income: −10%

• Middle Income: −20%

• High Income: −30%

## Dashboard Components

### Energy Consumption Analysis

• Monthly Usage KWH by Region

• Monthly Usage KWH by Country

• Monthly Usage KWH by Energy Source

### Cost Savings Analysis

• Cost Savings by Region

• Cost Savings by Country

• Cost Savings by Energy Source

## Key Insights

• Europe recorded the highest total renewable energy consumption.

• Australia and New Zealand ranked among the highest energy-consuming countries.

• Hydro energy generated the highest overall energy usage.

• Wind energy produced the highest overall cost savings.

• Regional variations indicate significant differences in renewable energy adoption and economic impact.
